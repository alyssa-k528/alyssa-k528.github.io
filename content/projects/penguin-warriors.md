I participated in the CMD-F hackathon in 2024. We won runner-up in the HealthTech for All track and the UBC CS Hub prize.

We created an interactive game that tracks exercise movements using TensorFlow and MoveNet. Players must complete exercises with accurate form to attack their enemy; otherwise, they will be attacked.
